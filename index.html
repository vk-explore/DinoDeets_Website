About Dino Deets

Hosted by Devaansh

Welcome to Dino Deets — where we travel millions of years back in time to uncover the most amazing secrets of the prehistoric world!

Every Friday, join Devaansh as he explores the lives of dinosaurs, digs into ancient fossils, uncovers Greek myths, chats with real experts, and shares mind-blowing facts that will make you the coolest kid at school.

Whether you're a dino-obsessed kid, a curious parent, or just someone who thinks T-Rex is awesome — this is YOUR podcast!
